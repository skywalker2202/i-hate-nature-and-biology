About this blog

This is a personal learning and research blog about computational biology. I use it to explain difficult concepts, document research ideas, and connect evidence across genetics, disease biology, and biomedical data.

The goal is not to make every topic simple by removing detail. It is to make the structure of the reasoning visible.
